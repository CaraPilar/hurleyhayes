Privacy Policy

We collect the information you voluntarily provide through our Hurley-Hayes Family Reunion interest and registration form, including your name, email address, and phone number.

This information is used solely for planning, organizing, and communicating about the Hurley-Hayes Family Reunion, including sending registration confirmations and event-related updates.

Information submitted through the registration form is accessible only to reunion organizers and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties except as necessary to operate the registration process.

We retain information only as long as needed for reunion planning and follow-up activities. You may request that your information be updated or removed by contacting us.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is used, please contact the reunion organizers.
